pitch schedule explorer

Hold pitch0 for n0 ticks, then pitch1 for n1, then pitch2 for n2, from v0. Physics is the f64 libm transcription of plot_field_replay.step, not mth_lut.

Text boxes take any value, past the slider's range too. State lives in the URL hash.

ΔTE is total energy in blocks: altitude plus |v|²/2g, with g = 0.08 b/t². Ends are at the last tick of each hold.

Drag v0 to move it, drag elsewhere to pan; wheel zooms, double-click resets. Hover either plot to mark a tick on both.
