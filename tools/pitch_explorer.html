Elytra pitch explorer

Fly a schedule of pitch segments from a starting velocity v0. Each segment moves its pitch linearly from start to end over its ticks, both ends included. Pitch is Minecraft's: negative is nose up. Physics is vanilla elytra flight at yaw 0, in double precision without the game's sine table.

Velocities are in blocks per tick. ΔTE is the gain in total energy since v0, in blocks: altitude plus |v|²/2g, with g = 0.08 b/t². ΔTE/n is that gain per tick.

Drag v0 to move it, drag elsewhere to pan; wheel zooms.

Drag a segment's line or its dots to change its pitch, and the triangles on the tick axis to move where segments switch or end. Pitch snaps to 1°; hold shift for 0.1°.
