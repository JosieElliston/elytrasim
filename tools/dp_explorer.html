Ablated DP explorer

pumpdp's discounted DP (horizon 3000) solved under one of two ablations of the descent-to-forward conversion. --conv-curv f scales the conversion's lift² coefficient and holds it fixed at lift 0 and 1. --conv-lift-exp k raises the conversion's outer lift factor to liftk. f = k = 1 is the game. Both sliders run toward more conversion at interior pitches. Drag the white dot (v0) to move it and fly that DP's policy from it; drag anywhere else to pan the map. The wheel (or a pinch) zooms around the pointer. Hover the map or the pitch trace to probe Q there. Pitch is Minecraft's, so positive is nose down.

Velocities in blocks per tick, energies in blocks (TE = altitude + |v|²/2g, g = 0.08). Q(v, p) = r + γV(v′), with r the tick's ΔTE and γ = 1 − 1/3000. V is pumpdp's, read bilinearly as pumpdp does. The policy and the flights take the argmax over pumpdp's 0.25° pitch grid on [−89, 89]. The maps use a 1° scan refined to 0.25°. V is embedded as float32. Against pumpdp's own flight in the game, that moves the pitch on 48 of 3000 ticks, by up to 3°, all where Q is nearly flat in pitch, and the flight ends on the same state. The lift panel plots Q over the dive branch p ∈ [0, 89] against lift = cos²p, minus the chord between the two bangs (p = 0 at lift 1, p = 89 at lift ≈ 0.0003). Q = ΔPE + ΔKE + γV(v′), with ΔPE = vy′ (PE is altitude); the panel draws each minus its own chord. In the dive ΔPE's only nonlinear term in lift is the conversion's. Under f, minus its chord ΔPE is one fixed parabola (gap 0.00147·f at the middle lift), whatever v is; under k the conversion's outer factor is liftk, and the bend it adds scales with the fall speed. Positive means concave, where an interior pitch beats mixing the bangs. The midpoint gap is that total at the middle lift (p ≈ 45°). A flip is a node whose policy flies 0↔89 then back across more than 40° each tick. With "fly the ablated policy on the game's physics" on, the pitch is still chosen from the ablated Q (the ablated physics and its V), but every flight, the flip map's ticks and the cycle row advance on the game's physics. A reversal is a tick where pitch turns around by more than 10° on both sides.
