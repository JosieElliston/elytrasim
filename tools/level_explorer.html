Level flight policy

Velocity → pitch policies solved by pumpdp with forward distance priced at λ: each tick's reward is ΔTE + w·Δz, w = λ·21.5/330 as in the sweeps. λ = 0 is the gain-rate pump; raising λ trades climb for forward speed, and the λ whose cycle climbs 0 is the fastest sustained level flight. Drag on the velocity plane to start a flight anywhere; the zoom follows the pointer over the plane and sits on v0 otherwise. Click the frontier to pick λ. Pitch is Minecraft's: negative is nose up, drawn up.

Velocities and rates are in blocks per tick (× 20 for blocks per second). Each policy is solved discounted with horizon 3000 on a 0.01 b/t grid. Flights pick each tick's pitch as pumpdp does, the quarter degree maximizing the tick's reward plus the discounted V of where it lands; the zoom draws that choice. The limit cycle is pumpdp's own 60000-tick flight from the hand-flown reference's v0, read over its last 50000 ticks. Climb is total-energy gain per tick, which over whole cycles is height gain. Height swing is the cycle's peak-to-trough height after removing its mean climb. ΔTE is altitude plus |v|²/2g, with g = 0.08 b/t². Physics k = 0.99 is pumpdp's --conv-lift-exp ablation, not Minecraft: the descent-to-forward conversion's outer lift factor becomes lift^k, which stops the vanilla policy's tick-rate chatter; maps and flights both use it, and the frontier draws the other physics' faintly.
