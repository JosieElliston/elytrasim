Pump policy blur

A velocity → pitch policy solved on a grid by pumpdp. Blurring its map with a Gaussian of width σ keeps it a function of velocity alone and trades climb rate for less chatter. Drag on the velocity plane to start a flight anywhere; the zoom below the flight path follows the pointer over the plane and sits on v0 otherwise. Drag in the zoom to pan it, which moves v0 with its center; the wheel over either plane sets the zoom's size. Pitch is Minecraft's: negative is nose up, drawn up.

this blur's limit cycle

rate and chatter against σ

Velocities are in blocks per tick; σ too (× 20 for blocks per second). The solution grid is 0.01 b/t unless named fine (0.005). The limit cycle is read off 30000 ticks flown from the hand-flown reference's v0, after a 10000-tick settle, over whole laps; a lap runs snap to snap, the snap being the first tick pitch reaches −80. Rate is total-energy gain per second. Reversals count ticks where pitch turns around by more than 2° each way. ΔTE is altitude plus |v|²/2g, with g = 0.08 b/t².
